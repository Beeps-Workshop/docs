Makes Netherite gear finite: on break it downgrades to the diamond equivalent, and it can't be topped up with Mending.

### Highlights
- **Minimum version bumped to 1.21.1** (now requires Java 17). Builds target 1.21.1–1.21.8.
- **Netherite Spear support** — handled like the rest of the netherite gear when the item exists on the server.
- **Mending XP is no longer wasted on netherite.** When a mending orb would repair a netherite item, the XP is redirected to your most-damaged Mending-enchanted item across your hands and armor. With no eligible item, you simply keep the XP.
