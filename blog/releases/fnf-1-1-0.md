**New: Copper Gutters:**
- New blocks: Gutter, Drain Pipe, Drain & Basin.
- Gutters connect with each other to form runs
- Place a drain pipe one block away from a gutter and connect it to a drain and it'll spray water when it rains!
- Place a basin or cauldron next to the drain and it'll fill up with rain faster than a cauldron in open weather
- Basin: Can be used to clear of dye from leather, last pattern off a banner and the color of a shulker

**Slats:**
- Pink petals can now be applied to slats, similar to vines
- When upgrading, existing slats will lose their vines (sorry)
- Logs now craft into 32 plank slats instead of 8
