# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-11-26_

Beeps' Workshop is a documentation site for Minecraft mods and plugins. There are no accounts, no
sign-ups and no ads, and the site doesn't set any cookies.

## Analytics

The site counts visits with [Umami](https://umami.is), which I host myself. Umami doesn't use cookies
and doesn't collect personal data: it records anonymous, aggregate numbers such as page views,
referrers, browser, device type and country. It can't be used to identify or track you across
sites, and the data isn't shared with anyone.

## Server logs

Like most websites, the server keeps standard access logs: IP address, browser (user agent), the page
requested and the time. They're used to keep the site running and secure, and aren't shared.

## Fonts

The title font on the homepage is served by [Adobe Fonts](https://fonts.adobe.com). Loading the
homepage sends your IP address and browser details to Adobe, which also counts font usage for
licensing. See [Adobe's privacy policy](https://www.adobe.com/privacy/policy.html). All other fonts
are served from this site.

## Contact form

The contact form on the [Terms](/terms#contact) page is embedded from Notion. Loading that page
connects to Notion, and anything you send through the form is stored by Notion under Notion's own
privacy policy.

## Sponsor link

The banner in the footer is an affiliate link to RealisticNode. If you click it and buy a server, I
may get a referral commission, at no extra cost to you. Clicking it takes you to RealisticNode's site,
where their own privacy policy applies; this site doesn't share anything with them.

## Other links

Links to Modrinth, CurseForge, GitHub, Discord and Ko-fi take you to those services, and their own
privacy policies apply there.

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information
from children.

## Your rights

If you have questions about this policy, or want to make a request about data collected through the
site, email [workshop@beeps.email](mailto:workshop@beeps.email).
