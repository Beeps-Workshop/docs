# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-23_

Beep's Workshop is a documentation site for mods and plugins. It doesn't require accounts or
sign-ups, and it doesn't collect personal data directly through the site.

## Advertising & cookies

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partner vendors use cookies to serve
ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized
advertising by visiting:

- [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com)
- [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/)

## Log data

Like most websites, our hosting provider may collect standard log data such as IP address,
browser type, pages visited, and timestamps.

## Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children.
