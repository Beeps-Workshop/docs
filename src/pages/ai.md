# AI Disclosure
Below is a little explainer on how AI (Artificial Intelligence) is used in my projects.
> TLDR: I use AI for code so I can focus on creative work.

## Disclosure
- ✅ Coding Assistance/Generation
- ✅ Translations & Proofreading
- ❌ Artwork, Sprites & Textures
- ❌ Music & Sound effects
- ❌ 3D Models & Animations
- ❌ Creative Writing

*Anything not on this list that you're curious about, feel free to ask me directly.*

## Why?
I'm a professional software engineer and have been for almost 10 years.
I use AI tools to do the boring parts of my job like writing boilerplate code, generating tests, and refactoring.
I remain the human in charge of all the designs, architecture, and implementation of the software I work on.

I refuse to use AI for creative works like writing, drawing or composing. I would much rather learn to do those myself
than rely on an AI to do the work for me.

Code is the exception to this, because I know how to write it, and thus I know how to probe an AI model to not make as many mistakes as a human would.
I also know how to read and understand code, and thus I can easily spot mistakes in AI generated code.

Using these tools lets me use the precious little bit of free time I have to focus on the more enjoyable creative aspects of these projects.
**If you do not wish to use/enjoy my projects because of this, I understand and respect your decision.**
