# Troubleshooting
:::info
this section is a work in progress and will be expanded over time
:::

## Is the mod loaded?
Check the mods list in-game (or your launcher) for BMCRM. If it's missing, double-check you're
running **NeoForge** (not Forge or Fabric) for **Minecraft 1.21.1**, and that the jar is in your
`mods` folder.

## Crystals aren't generating anything
Big chance you are just getting unlucky, Glass crystals only have a 4% chance to generate anything.
However, it could also be caused by a bug, so check these before anything:
- Does the crystal look correct in the inventory? If you are seeing weird tooltips that is a telltale sign something is wrong.
- Did you blacklist any ores in the config? If so, make sure you didn't blacklist anything you're currently using.

## Check your logs!
Check your console/log for errors, especially on world load. The `latest.log` file in your
instance's `logs` folder may have more detail on what went wrong.
