# Membrane

A slime-like block that generates in blobs on the outer end isles.

## Behavior
Stepping into a Membrane block slows your horizontal movement to 80% — it's sticky enough to slow
you down, but won't drag you along with piston movement like a real slime block does.

## Where to find it
Membrane generates in small clustered blobs on the outer end isles, usually alongside
[Membrane Bubble](./MembraneBubble.md) blocks.
