# Small Storage Net

A net slung from the ceiling holding a single chest's worth of items — 27 slots.

## Placing it
Right-click the **underside** of a block. It's ceiling-mounted only; walls and floors won't take
one.

Like every fixture it snaps to the shape above it, so hanging one under a slab or a trapdoor rests
it against that surface rather than the block boundary.

## Using it
Right-click to open it. It uses the normal chest screen, so it behaves exactly as you'd expect.

A few of the items inside are visible resting in the net, so you can tell a stocked net from an
empty one at a glance without opening it.

Breaking the net drops it and everything inside.

## Crafting
Eight string in a ring, the same shape as a chest.
