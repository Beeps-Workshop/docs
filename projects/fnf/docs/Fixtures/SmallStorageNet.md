# Small Storage Net

A net slung from the ceiling holding a single chest's worth of items.

![Storage nets hung from a ceiling](/img/projects/fnf/ceiling-nets.png)

## Placing it
Right-click the **underside** of a block. It's ceiling-mounted only.
one.

Like every fixture it snaps to the shape above it, so hanging one under a slab or a trapdoor rests
it against that surface rather than the block boundary.

## Using it
The net stores the same amount of items as a normal chest.

A few of the items inside are visible resting in the net, so you can tell a stocked net from an
empty one at a glance without opening it.

Breaking the net drops it and everything inside.
