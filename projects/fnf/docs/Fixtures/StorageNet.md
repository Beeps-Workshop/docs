# Storage Net

A net slung along a wall holding a double chest's worth of items — 54 slots.

![Wall-mounted storage nets](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/giBTRDbb/images/dc14d4d22d2d810b4a53fa60ec4fa19fb157127f.png)

## Placing it
Right-click a **wall**. It hangs from that one block but is wider than it, overhanging to either
side, so it reads as a proper two-block hammock without you having to place it twice.

Because it's wider than a block, **two can't sit on adjacent blocks** — their nets would overlap.
Leave a block between them.

Only the block you attach it to needs to be solid, so a net can overhang the end of a wall if you
want it to.

## Using it
Right-click to open it. It uses the normal double chest screen.

Five of the items inside rest visibly in the net — more than the small one shows, since there's
more net to fill.

Breaking it drops the net and everything inside.

## Crafting
Two Small Storage Nets side by side, the same way a double chest is two chests.
