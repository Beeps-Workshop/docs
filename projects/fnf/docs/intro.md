## Fixtures

Fixtures are placed onto a block face the way an item frame is, but they snap to the *shape* of
whatever you attach them to instead of standing off it. Hang one on slats, a fence or a trapdoor
and it sits flush against the surface rather than floating a block away.

- **[Wide Planter](./Fixtures/WidePlanter.md)**: A wall-mounted trough holding three plants side by side.
- **[Small Storage Net](./Fixtures/SmallStorageNet.md)**: A ceiling-hung net with a chest's worth of storage.
- **[Storage Net](./Fixtures/StorageNet.md)**: A wall-hung net, two blocks wide, with a double chest's worth.

![Wall-mounted storage nets](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/giBTRDbb/images/dc14d4d22d2d810b4a53fa60ec4fa19fb157127f.png)

## Blocks

- **[Slats](./Blocks/Slats.md)**: Modern slat panelling in 39 variants — every wood, plus glowstone,
  sea lantern, the three froglights and magma. Mount them on walls or stand them on the floor as a
  partition.
- **[Sheetmetal](./Blocks/Sheetmetal.md)**: Corrugated copper and iron that rotates to face you, so
  runs of it line up into patterns.

![All the slats](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/giBTRDbb/images/2c40d95c10d78d5a5de8607a563c3ebb8dd6cdc2.png)
