**_A Small decorative mod for you and yours._**

## Fixtures
![Separator](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/67b87a7b35c2463aa362c262d80c26da971d7c51.png)
Fixtures are placed onto a block face the way an item frame is, but they snap to the *shape* of
whatever you attach them to instead of standing off it. Hang one on slats, a fence or a trapdoor
and it sits flush against the surface rather than floating a block away.

- **Wide Planter**: A wall-mounted trough holding three plants side by side.
- **Small Storage Net**: A ceiling-hung net with a chest's worth of storage.
- **Storage Net**: A wall-hung net, two blocks wide, with a double chest's worth.

## Blocks
![Separator](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/67b87a7b35c2463aa362c262d80c26da971d7c51.png)
- **Slats**: Modern slat panelling in 39 variants — every wood, plus glowstone, sea lantern, the
  three froglights and magma. Mount them on walls or stand them on the floor as a partition.
- **Sheetmetal**: Corrugated copper and iron that rotates to face you, so runs of it line up into
  patterns.
