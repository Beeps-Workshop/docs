# Auto-Smelt Modulator

Automatically smelts whatever the Resonator generates.

## Area of effect
Unlike the other modulators, Auto-Smelt has no area around itself. Instead, it checks whether the
straight line between a crystal and its Resonator passes through the modulator's block — so it
has to sit directly between the two, not just nearby.

## Notes
The modulator glows and will ignite entities that stand on it, so place it somewhere out of the
way of foot traffic.
