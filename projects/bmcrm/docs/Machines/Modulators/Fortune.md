# Fortune Modulator

Grants Fortune to the Resonator's breaks.

## Area of effect
Covers a flat "+" shape, 2 blocks out in each direction from the modulator (so a 5-long arm each
way, crossing at the modulator) — not a full 5×5 square.

## Stacking
Each Fortune Modulator that covers a crystal adds +1 effective Fortune level. The total is capped
by the `maxFortuneLevel` config option (5 by default; 0 means uncapped) — see
[Configuration](../../Configuration.md#maxfortunelevel).
