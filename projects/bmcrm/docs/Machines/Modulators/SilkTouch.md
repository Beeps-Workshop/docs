# Silk Touch Modulator

Makes the Resonator break its ore blocks with Silk Touch instead of a normal drop.

## Area of effect
Covers a flat 3×3 area directly in front of the modulator (not a full cube — it doesn't reach up
or down). Any crystal within that area is affected.

## Notes
Silk Touch always wins over a [Fortune Modulator](./Fortune.md) if both somehow cover the same
crystal — you get one block, not a Fortune-boosted drop.
