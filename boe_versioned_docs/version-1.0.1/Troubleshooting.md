# Troubleshooting
:::info
this section is a work in progress and will be expanded over time
:::

## Is the mod loaded?
Check the mods list in-game (or your launcher) for Beep's Other End. If it's missing, double-check
you're running **Forge** (not NeoForge or Fabric) for **Minecraft 1.20.1**, and that the jar is in
your `mods` folder.

## New End content isn't generating
Content like Membrane and Enlyum only generates in **new** end island chunks. visiting already-generated islands won't retroactively add it. Try a fresh end island or a new world.

## Check your logs!
Check your console/log for errors, especially on world load. The `latest.log` file in your
instance's `logs` folder may have more detail on what went wrong.

## Still having issues?
Feel free to join the [Discord](https://beepsterr.com/discord) for help!
