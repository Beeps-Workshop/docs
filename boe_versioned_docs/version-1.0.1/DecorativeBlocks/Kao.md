# Kao

A purplish take on Oak that generates naturally in the End.

## Growing your own
Plant a Kao Sapling on End Stone, dirt, or farmland — it grows into a real tree over time, just
like a vanilla sapling.

## Natural generation
Kao trees generate on their own in the End Highlands and Midlands, including occasional small
clusters — you don't have to plant every one yourself.

## Button & pressure plate
These behave a little differently from vanilla:
- **Kao Button** — normally sits powered. Right-clicking it depowers it briefly (1.5 seconds)
  before it flips back on, rather than a normal momentary press.
- **Kao Pressure Plate** — stepping on it powers it, but unlike a vanilla plate it stays powered
  after you step off. Right-click it to turn it back off.
