# Nightmare Bell

A bell found within the Shelter — ring it at your own risk.

## Ringing it
Like a normal bell, it rings if you right-click it or hit it with a projectile. Doing so inflicts
the Nightmare effect on you for 30 seconds.

## Nightmare effect
While it's active, aggressive Phantoms spawn above and around you every 5 seconds, immediately
targeting you. There's no known way to remove it early — just survive until it wears off.
