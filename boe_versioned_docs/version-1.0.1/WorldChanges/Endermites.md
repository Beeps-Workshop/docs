# Endermites & Infested End Stone

Endermites behave differently in the End now.

## Merging into stone
An idle Endermite will occasionally burrow into a nearby End Stone block, turning it into
(visually identical) Infested End Stone and disappearing. Breaking Infested End Stone without Silk
Touch pops a fresh Endermite back out — so that "ordinary" End Stone you're mining might not be so
ordinary.

## Waking up friends
Hurting an Endermite eventually causes it to search a large area around itself for Infested End
Stone and either destroy it or hatch a new Endermite from it, with a chance to chain into more —
attacking one Endermite near infested stone can turn into a small swarm.
